The proposal in brief

Today each carrier statement is typed into an Excel reconciliation by hand, and "mark as paid" is clicked on individual release notes. A payment is made against a carrier invoice, not a release note, so the two never line up cleanly.

We propose a new Carrier Invoices screen where each carrier invoice is captured once, and a new Carrier Payments screen that works like a statement of what Container Deals owes each carrier. It shows what has been invoiced, what has been paid and what is still outstanding, month by month. Payments are ticked off on that screen, and the "Mark as Paid" button on the release note goes away.

What changes

Today
  • The carrier emails a statement. Someone types each invoice into Excel.
  • Invoice numbers are captured per container, but nothing checks the carrier's invoice against them.
  • "Mark as Paid" is clicked per release note, which may cover several invoices or only part of one.
  • Outstanding balances are worked out in Excel and printed.
With this proposal
  • Each carrier invoice is captured once, and the system checks it against the containers you authorised.
  • A wrong number or a wrong amount is rejected immediately, with a clear message.
  • Payment is recorded per carrier invoice, in one place, after you pay.
  • Invoiced, paid and outstanding are always up to date per carrier and per month, with a printable statement.

How it works

Flow 1. Capture a carrier invoice

Carrier Invoices screen
Invoice arrives

The carrier's tax invoice arrives by email or on paper.

Enter the invoice

Choose the carrier. Enter the invoice number exactly as printed, the invoice date and the invoice total including VAT.

The system checks

Are there authorised containers for this carrier with this invoice number? Once VAT is added to their fees at the current system rate, does the result equal the invoice total entered? Has it been captured already?

If any check fails, nothing is saved and the message says why.
Saved

The invoice is saved with the total exactly as entered. The invoice date and the cut-off day in System Settings decide which month's list it goes on.

The invoice now counts as owed.

Flow 2. Pay and reconcile for the month

Carrier Payments screen
Open the month

Pick the carrier and the month. A month ends on the cut-off day set in System Settings. See invoiced, paid, outstanding, plus anything brought forward from earlier months.

Compare to the statement

Check the list against the carrier's emailed statement. Anything authorised but not yet captured is listed separately.

Pay in your bank

Make the EFT as you do today. The system does not move money.

Mark as paid

Tick the invoices you paid, enter the payment date and an optional bank reference, and confirm.

Totals update

The invoices stay on the month, now marked Paid with their payment date. Paid and outstanding totals update straight away.

Print the statement

Print the reconciliation for the carrier and month, laid out like your current Excel sheet.

Flow 3. Release note status follows the payment

Automatic
Invoice is marked paid

The system looks at every release note that has containers on that invoice.

All containers paid?

If every container on the release note (charity cargo excluded) sits on a paid invoice, the release note becomes Paid.

Paid release notes appear in the Paid tab as today.
Otherwise

The release note stays in Pending Payment until its remaining invoices are paid.

Paid by mistake?

An administrator can reverse a payment on the Carrier Payments screen. The release notes go back to Pending Payment. The reversal is recorded.

Invoice or release note deleted

Only the Superuser account can do this. If a carrier invoice is deleted, or a release note on it is deleted, the user is warned first. The carrier invoice is removed and the other release notes on it go back to Pending Payment.

Screen 7 · Phase 1

System Settings: cut-off day and start date

A new setting that decides which month's list a carrier invoice goes on. Without it, an invoice dated 26 August sits on August's list and only shows as brought forward in September, so you would go back to August to mark it paid. With a cut-off of 25, it lands on September's list with the other invoices in the same payment cycle. A second setting, the carrier payments start date, keeps old history out of the "Authorised but not yet captured" lists.

Rules the system enforces

RuleWhat happens
Invoice number must existThe number entered must already be on authorised containers for that carrier. If not, capture is refused: "No containers authorised for the invoice number."
Amount must matchThe invoice total entered includes VAT. The system adds up the transporter fees of those containers, adds VAT at the current system rate, and the result must equal the invoice total, to the cent. If not, capture is refused: "The invoice amount does not match the sum of the authorised containers for the invoice number."
One capture per invoiceA carrier invoice number can be captured once per carrier.
VATVAT is used only for the check above. The captured invoice keeps the total exactly as entered, so a later change to the system VAT rate does not alter captured invoices. Carriers not registered for VAT are checked without VAT.
Month and cut-off dayThe invoice date and the cut-off day in System Settings decide the month's list. An invoice dated on or before the cut-off day goes on that month's list. An invoice dated after it goes on the next month's list. The default cut-off is the last day of the month, which gives normal calendar months. The list is fixed when the invoice is captured. Unpaid invoices from earlier months show as brought forward until paid.
Start dateThe carrier payments start date in System Settings hides history from "Authorised but not yet captured" and the Carrier Overview. Release notes delivered before it are left out, and release notes already marked paid are never listed. It does not hide invoices already captured, and does not stop an invoice being captured. Leave it empty to include everything.
LockingOnce an invoice is captured, the invoice number and fee on its containers cannot be changed, and no further containers can be given that invoice number. To change them, edit the unpaid carrier invoice (the checks run again) or delete it.
PaymentsInvoices are paid in full. Partial payments are not supported. Once an invoice is marked as paid it cannot be edited, including its invoice number, date and amount. To change it, an administrator reverses the payment first, and who did that and when is recorded.
Deleting a carrier invoiceCan only be done by the Superuser account, as for invoices today, using the same warning dialog. The dialog lists what will be affected. The carrier invoice is removed, and if it was paid its payment is removed too. Every release note on it moves back to Pending Payment. The containers keep their invoice number, so it returns to "Authorised but not yet captured".
Deleting a release note on a carrier invoiceRelease notes can only be deleted by the Superuser account today, and that does not change. The existing delete dialog warns that the carrier invoice will be removed, and that any other release notes on that invoice will move back to Pending Payment. If the carrier invoice was paid, its payment is removed too.
Charity cargoCharity cargo containers carry no invoice and never appear in Carrier Payments.
AccessThe Carrier Payments menu and its screens are visible to administrators only, with no extra PIN prompt. Capturing and editing invoices and marking them paid is limited to administrators, the same as "Mark as Paid" today. Deleting a carrier invoice, or a release note on one, can only be done by the Superuser account, as the rest of the system enforces for invoices and release notes.

Existing data and screens

Release notes already marked paid

They stay Paid and are not touched. They do not appear in Carrier Payments, because no carrier invoice was captured for them.

Invoices unpaid at go-live

The carrier invoices currently unpaid need to be captured once from the latest statements, so that Carrier Payments starts with the correct outstanding balance. We will support this during go-live.

Dashboards and reports

Release note Paid status continues to drive the existing Paid tab, dashboard and reports. Nothing else needs to change for those to keep working.

Scope

Included
  • Carrier Invoices screen with capture, validation, list, and edit (unpaid only)
  • Deleting a carrier invoice (Superuser account only), with a warning of what is affected and release notes returned to Pending Payment
  • Deleting a release note that is on a carrier invoice, with the same warning
  • VAT-inclusive check of the invoice total against the container fees, at the current system rate
  • Locking of invoice numbers and fees once captured
  • Carrier invoice cut-off day and carrier payments start date settings under System Settings
  • A "Carrier Payments" menu group (Overview, Carrier Invoices, Carrier Payments) for administrators
  • Carrier Payments screen by carrier and month, with brought-forward balances
  • Carrier Overview screen showing every carrier's balance for the month, linking through to each carrier's payments
  • List of authorised invoice numbers not yet captured
  • Mark as paid and reverse payment, with audit trail
  • Release note Paid status driven by carrier invoice payments, and removal of the old button
  • Printable reconciliation statement
  • Automated tests, user guide update, user acceptance testing and go-live support
Not included now
  • Demurrage invoices. These are invoices Container Deals sends to carriers, so the carrier owes the money. They can be added later as a credit line on the carrier's statement, with the demurrage details.
  • Partial payments
  • Importing or reading the carrier's emailed statement automatically
  • Comparing a carrier's statement total to the system and showing the difference
  • Making bank payments from the system
  • Anything new on the Dashboard
